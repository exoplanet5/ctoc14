## Exact-conversion check (2026-09-15, lead)

The two tours with the largest planned saving were converted with `tools/convert_one.py` at the SAME tank as their
original conversion (`results/retime/check_camp*_sc*.txt`, both validator PASS):

| tour | planned fuel before -> after re-timing | original conversion | re-timed conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| camp1 sc5 (28 legs, m0 1086) | 390.5 -> 298.6 kg (-92) | 28 flybys, 447.1 kg | **26 flybys** (dropped 25, 163), 470.9 kg |
| camp2 sc3 (30 legs, m0 1211) | 502.1 -> 402.1 kg (-100) | 30 flybys, 500.4 kg | 30 flybys, 495.3 kg (-5) |

**Verdict: the 15-16 % planned saving of plan-level re-timing is a planner-model artefact.** The coordinate descent
exploits the hypersensitivity of single-revolution Lambert junctions to the flyby times; the converter already optimises
the flyby times itself (Newton on thrust samples + times), so the re-timed plan converts no cheaper (camp2 sc3) or
worse (camp1 sc5: two flybys lost). Do not use re-timing of planned tours as a fuel lever; the sequence is what the
converter inherits. (Consistent with docs/conversion_losses.md section 2 on Lambert-chain sensitivity.)
